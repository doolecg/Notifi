The resting pill: six options

Read this first

  1. Each mockup is the pill on your real taskbar (its left and right ends are from a screenshot of it), at its real height of 32 px.
  2. The numbers shown are the live ones: weather 8°, CPU 8%, RAM 33%, AI 54%, two notifications.
  3. Pick a letter, or mix: "A, but with the rings from F".

My pick: A. It drops the cramped row of three prompt chips for one Ask AI button, and the rings read at a glance. Everything else about the pill stays.
