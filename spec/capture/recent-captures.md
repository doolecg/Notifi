# Recent captures

A history of recent screenshots and recordings.

## User-visible behaviour
- Opens from the panel or by a shortcut; shows thumbnails of recent captures and recordings with open, copy, reveal, delete and edit actions.
- Clear the whole history from the header.
- History is stored privately; unreadable lists never lose the images.

## Settings
- Keep last N captures (default 50); shortcut (default none)

## Default shortcuts
None.

## Edge cases
- Images are preserved if the list cannot be read or saved.

## Permissions
None.

## Platform notes
Same on both platforms.
- Status: Windows PORT, Linux PORT.
