# Companion (Top Pill mascot)

An optional little character that sits next to the Top Pill, responds to events, and can bring the Command Bar out of the pill.

## User-visible behaviour
- Stays tucked in the pill and emerges to respond to music, timers, AI agents, downloads and notifications.
- It can optionally stay hidden in the pill during quiet periods and pop out now and then.
- Optional: the Command Bar shortcut drops the bar out of the pill with the companion as its face, and takes it back when closed.
- Own look and character (Notifi's, not Vorssaint's).
- Respects reduced motion.

## Settings
- Enable (default off); hide when idle (default on); Command Bar in the pill (default off)

## Default shortcuts
Uses the Command Bar shortcut.

## Edge cases
- Companion never blocks clicks on the pill; hidden when the pill is hidden.

## Permissions
None.

## Platform notes
Same on both platforms; pure UI.
- Status: Windows PORT, Linux PORT.
